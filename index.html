Questions

Before you come up.

Anything else, call (855) 999-1087 and a leasing team member will pick up during office hours.

Is a security deposit required?

No. 65 Bay Street offers move-in with no security deposit through Jetty. Ask the leasing team how it applies to your application.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Cats and dogs are welcome, up to two per apartment, with breed restrictions. Pets must be pre-approved and house trained, with a recent veterinarian statement of good health and a photo. There is a non-refundable $500 fee per pet and $50 per month.

Is parking available?

Yes. Residents can use discounted covered parking in the garage, which has EV parking.

Are furnished apartments offered?

No. 65 Bay Street does not offer furnished rentals.

How far is the PATH?

Exchange Place PATH is half a mile away, about a 7-minute ride to downtown Manhattan. Grove Street PATH is about a mile.

Do I need an appointment to tour?

Appointments are encouraged and walk-ins are welcome. Tours are available in person or virtually.
